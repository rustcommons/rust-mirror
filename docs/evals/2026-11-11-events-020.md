## Deliberate limitations

Synchronous blocked I/O may delay cancellation until it returns or times out;
independent heartbeats and the GUI watchdog/grace/kill policy expose that distinction.
A killed process or blocked/broken stdout can lack a terminal event. A heartbeat
reports latest declared work, not proven progress/freshness. Observers must be quick
and nonreentrant. New-output assembly/finishing preserves inputs rather than
claiming in-place resume. Publish/prune may have partial effects, documented in
EVENTS.md and RETENTION.md. Known locks do not certify hostile/shared filesystems,
untracked writers or globally atomic remote activation. No remote pruning is added.

G5 exact config-aware run inspection is deferred; existing metadata-only retention
discovery does not prove full resume compatibility. G1/G2/G4/G7 are implemented in
this candidate and remain subject to the qualification/publication gates.
